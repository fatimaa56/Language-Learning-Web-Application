🇫🇷 Learn French - A1 Level

Learning French can be an exciting journey! Start with basic greetings like Bonjour (Hello) and Merci (Thank you). Practice common phrases daily to build confidence in speaking. Listening to French songs or watching shows can improve your pronunciation. Remember, consistency is key to mastering the language!

Greetings

Bonjour - Hello

Bonne journée - Good day

Bonsoir - Good evening

Au revoir - Goodbye

À bientôt - See you soon

Basic Sentences

Je m'appelle... - My name is...

Je viens de... - I come from...

Je parle un peu français. - I speak a little French.

Quelle heure est-il ? - What time is it?

Pouvez-vous m'aider ? - Can you help me?

Common Phrases

Comment ça va ? - How are you?

Merci - Thank you

Pardon - Excuse me

S'il vous plaît - Please

Je ne comprends pas - I don't understand
