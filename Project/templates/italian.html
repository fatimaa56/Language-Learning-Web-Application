🇮🇹 Learn Italian - A1 Level

Learning Italian is a wonderful experience! Start with basic greetings like Ciao (Hello) and Grazie (Thank you). Practice daily conversations to improve fluency. Listening to Italian music or watching movies can help you catch the rhythm of the language. Consistency is the key to becoming confident in Italian!

Greetings

Ciao - Hello

Buongiorno - Good day

Buonasera - Good evening

Arrivederci - Goodbye

A presto - See you soon

Basic Sentences

Mi chiamo... - My name is...

Vengo da... - I come from...

Parlo un po' d'italiano. - I speak a little Italian.

Che ore sono? - What time is it?

Può aiutarmi? - Can you help me?

Common Phrases

Come stai? - How are you?

Grazie - Thank you

Scusa - Excuse me

Per favore - Please

Non capisco - I don't understand
